Commercial Maintenance — Duval Landing Community

From prospect to a signed commercial maintenance agreement (July 2026)
  • Opportunity: identified a commercial community needing recurring grounds and exterior maintenance.
  • Proposal: priced a core maintenance program with optional add-on services (pressure washing and fencing, tree trimming, landscape bed restoration, sidewalk crack treatment).
  • Cross-selling: structured add-ons so the client could expand scope over time.
  • Outcome: commercial maintenance agreement signed July 2026. See the full operations case study and quoted pricing →
